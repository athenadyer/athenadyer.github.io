Athena Dyer

I am a sophomore journalism student with a passion for storytelling. Since diving into journalism during the fall of my freshman year in 2025, I've loved covering stories that may fly under the radar and connecting with diverse and ever-evolving communities. I strive for truthful and accurate coverage in every piece that I write even if that means being tough. My creative background began back in 2021 during lockdown when I first picked up video editing. A skill that now fuels my digital storytelling and ultimate career goal of becoming a sports broadcaster, specifically in the National Football League (NFL). With more sports stories on the horizon this January, I am excited to keep growing as a multi-platform journalist.
